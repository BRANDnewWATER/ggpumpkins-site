How Much Does Fall Porch Decorating Cost in Hickory, NC?

DIY vs. professional styling — what you'll actually spend, and what you get for the money

By Gorgeous Gourds  ·  Updated May 2026  ·  ← Back to Blog

Fall porch decorating looks simple until you start pricing it out. A trip to the farm stand turns into three trips. The hay bale you pictured doesn't quite work with the porch steps. The pumpkins you picked looked great at the farm but rot by Columbus Day.

Here's an honest breakdown of what fall porch decorating actually costs in Hickory, NC — whether you go DIY or hire someone to handle it.

A Hickory Colonial porch before and after professional styling — Royal Gourd package.

The Short Answer

A DIY fall porch display in Hickory runs $195–$350 in materials, plus 4–6 hours of your time across the season. Professional styling runs $275–$1,350 depending on porch size, and includes delivery, installation, and (optional) removal. Most Hickory homeowners who go professional choose the Royal Gourd at $525.

What DIY Actually Costs

Most people underestimate DIY fall decorating. Here's a realistic breakdown for a full porch display sourced locally near Hickory:

That's the materials cost. Add in your time: one to two hours shopping and hauling, one hour styling (longer if you want it to look right), and an hour of cleanup in November when the pumpkins go soft. You're looking at 4–6 hours across the season.

The other hidden cost: pumpkins sitting in a farm stand or home improvement store since early September don't last as long. If you're decorating in early October, you might get three weeks before things start looking sad.

Is It Worth Hiring a Pro?

That depends on what you're optimizing for.

DIY makes sense if you enjoy the shopping experience, want total creative control, or have a small porch that only needs 8–10 pumpkins. A $200 trip to a local farm stand gets the job done for a simple stoop display.

Professional styling makes sense if you want a full, proportional display that looks like it belongs on your specific porch — not just pumpkins placed on a step. It also makes sense if your time is worth more than the price difference, or if you've tried DIY and it never came out looking quite right.

The thing most people don't account with DIY: porch displays look best when the pumpkins are varied in size, shape, and color — and sourcing that variety requires multiple stops. We source directly from local North Carolina farms, which means fresher pumpkins, better selection, and displays that hold up through Thanksgiving.

How Long Do the Pumpkins Last?

On a covered porch in North Carolina, pumpkins typically last 4–8 weeks — much longer than displays in direct sun or on the ground. We install right before your delivery date, so the clock doesn't start until the day they go on your porch.

If any pumpkin goes bad within the first 14 days, we replace it at no charge. Just send us a photo.
