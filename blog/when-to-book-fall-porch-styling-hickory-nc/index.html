The Full Booking Timeline

You can save on a fall porch display two ways: time the booking right, or skip the steps that cost extra.

  • Book before August 1 for 10% off your total. On a Signature Porch package that's around $50 saved. On a Royal Gourd it's about $52. The discount comes off the final balance at installation — the $100 deposit stays the same.
  • Choose a weekday install. Weekday slots are easier to get the closer we get to fall, and they don't run into the same scheduling crunch as Saturdays.

Combine both and you get the best date you want at the best price we offer.

Why October Weekends Book First

Everyone wants the same thing: a styled porch for the peak of fall, when the weather is right and the neighborhood is at its most active. In Hickory, that window is usually the first two weekends of October. The leaves are turning, kids are trick-or-treating within a few weeks, and the air is cool enough to actually enjoy being outside.

That's also exactly when every other customer wants their install. There are only so many weekend slots on the calendar, and they go fast.

When Do the Pumpkins Look Best?

Our install season opens Labor Day weekend (September 7). On a covered porch in North Carolina, longevity depends on when in the season you install:

  • A Labor Day or September install looks great for 3–4 weeks — add our Mid-Season Refresh in late October to keep it peak through Halloween and Thanksgiving
  • An early October install looks great through Thanksgiving
  • A mid-October install holds well into late November
  • A late October install may start to fade before the season ends — the later you install, the shorter the window

NC heat in September shortens pumpkin life more than most people expect — that's why we apply a protective prep treatment to every install and offer the Mid-Season Refresh for early-season customers. We also source directly from North Carolina farms right before your delivery date, not weeks ahead sitting in a lot.

If any pumpkin goes bad within 14 days of install, we replace it at no charge. Just send us a photo. That's the guarantee.
