Why October Weekends Book First

Everyone wants the same thing: a styled porch for the peak of fall, when the weather is right and the neighborhood is at its most active. In Hickory, that window is usually the first two weekends of October. The leaves are turning, kids are trick-or-treating within a few weeks, and the air is cool enough to actually enjoy being outside.

That's also exactly when every other customer wants their install. There are only so many weekend slots on the calendar, and they go fast once booking opens.

When Do the Pumpkins Look Best?

On a covered porch in North Carolina, fresh pumpkins last 4–8 weeks. That means:

  • An early October install looks great through Thanksgiving
  • A mid-October install holds well into late November
  • A late October install may start to fade before the season ends

We source directly from North Carolina farms right before your install date — not weeks ahead of time sitting in a lot. That's the biggest reason our displays hold up longer than most DIY setups.

If any pumpkin goes bad within 14 days of install, we replace it at no charge. Just send us a photo. That's the guarantee.
