Which Gorgeous Gourds Package Fits Your Hickory Porch?

A straightforward way to choose the right display without overthinking it

By Gorgeous Gourds  ·  Updated May 2026  ·  ← Back to Blog

The real buying question is not "how do I style a porch myself?" It is "which package is enough for my home to look right?" That is where most people get stuck.

If the display is too small, the porch looks unfinished. If it is too big, it feels crowded. This guide is here to help Hickory-area homeowners choose the right Gorgeous Gourds package based on porch size, home style, and the kind of result they want.

A balanced porch display sized to the home instead of guessed at from a package photo alone.

Start With Porch Size, Not Price

The biggest mistake people make is choosing by budget first and scale second. A cheaper package that is too small for the porch does not save money if the result feels underwhelming. Start with what the porch needs visually, then decide whether that is worth it to you.

Classic Porch ($275): best for small stoops, townhomes, and tighter entries that need clean seasonal curb appeal without taking over the whole porch.
Royal Gourd ($525): best for standard covered porches and most Hickory homes. This is the safest choice when you want the porch to feel full and professionally styled.
Grand Gourd ($800): best for wider porches, more steps, or homes where one small cluster would get visually lost.
Signature Porch ($1,350): best for statement homes, broad front entries, and customers who want the porch to be a centerpiece through the season.

Which Package Fits Most Hickory Homes?

For a typical covered porch in Hickory, Conover, or Newton, Royal Gourd is usually the right answer. It gives enough pumpkin count, color, and layering to look intentional without jumping to a luxury-size install.

If your porch is narrow or shallow, Classic usually gets the job done. If your porch is wide, deep, or built to show off the house, Grand is where the display starts to feel proportional.

When the Ivory Crown Upgrade Makes Sense

If your home has darker siding, painted brick, cedar, stone, or a cleaner modern look, the Ivory Crown all-white upgrade can be the better move. It feels more refined and tends to photograph better on neutral exteriors than a traditional orange-heavy mix.

What If You Want Family Photos Too?

If the porch also needs to work for family photos, school pictures, or holiday hosting, that matters. The goal is not just a pile of pumpkins. It is a display that frames the porch and still leaves room to move naturally through the entry.

That is another reason to text a porch photo first. We can recommend a package that fits the house and the way you actually plan to use the space.

The Fastest Way to Choose Correctly

Send a porch photo. We can tell from the width, step count, and exterior style whether you need Classic, Royal, Grand, or Signature. That gets you to the right package faster than comparing pumpkin counts in a vacuum.

  • Classic Porch ($275): small stoops, narrow entries, townhomes
  • Royal Gourd ($525): standard covered porches, most Hickory homes
  • Grand Gourd ($800): wider porches that need multiple groupings
  • Signature Porch ($1,350): statement homes or large front entries
