Common Questions About Packages

Quick answers before you commit

Can I customize my package?

Absolutely. All packages can be upgraded with the Ivory Crown (all-white pumpkins, +20%). You can also add design setup on Classic and Royal, and removal service on any package. Want something completely custom? Text us.

Do I need to be home for installation?

Nope. We work on your front porch while you're out. Just make sure we have access to the porch area. We'll send you a photo of the finished display once we're done.

What if a pumpkin goes bad early?

We'll replace any pumpkin that goes bad within the first 14 days of install, no charge. Just send us a photo.

What's the $100 deposit for?

The $100 deposit reserves your spot on our calendar and is applied toward your total balance. Remaining balance is due at installation. Deposits are refundable if you cancel 14+ days before your scheduled install.
