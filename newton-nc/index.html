Local Delivery, Local Sourcing

We source our pumpkins from local North Carolina farms — fresh, just before your install date. Whether you're on Main Avenue, near Newton-Conover High, or in one of the newer neighborhoods off Highway 16, we deliver to your door and style it on the spot. You don't have to be home.
