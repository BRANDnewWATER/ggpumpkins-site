# Gorgeous Gourds Design Guide

## Product

Gorgeous Gourds is a local seasonal porch styling business near Hickory, North Carolina. The website should make visitors understand that the service creates premium fall porch displays with real pumpkins and gourds, delivered and styled for them.

Primary conversion:
- Text or email a porch photo
- Join the waitlist
- Reserve a spot with a deposit

## Brand Feel

The site should feel upscale, warm, local, polished, trustworthy, autumnal, and residential. It should feel premium without becoming corporate.

Avoid:
- Spooky Halloween styling
- Jack-o-lanterns or carved faces
- Cartoon pumpkins
- Fake plastic shine
- Overly perfect fantasy homes
- Cluttered walkways
- Heavy corn stalks dominating the display

## Visual Direction

Use realistic North Carolina residential porch photography. Images should look like attractive iPhone customer photos from a previous fall season: believable homes, real shadows, porch lights, shrubs, railings, doors, steps, doormats, and natural scale.

The core product is pumpkins and gourds. Add-ons such as mums, hay bales, and corn stalks may appear, but they should support the display rather than take over.

Hero images should:
- Show the actual porch display clearly
- Leave a darker or calmer central area for page title text
- Keep walkways visibly open
- Use warm daylight or golden-hour light

## Content

Copy should be clear, warm, local, and simple. Prefer practical language over exaggerated sales language.

Use:
- "porch styling"
- "fall porch display"
- "pumpkins and gourds"
- "Hickory-area homes"
- "text a photo of your porch"

Avoid:
- "install porches"
- Halloween language
- fabricated scarcity
- unsupported personal claims

## Accessibility

Pages should keep readable contrast over hero imagery using a dark overlay. Image alt text should describe the actual image and business context. Navigation must remain keyboard and mobile usable.

Button and link text should describe the action, such as "View Packages" or "Reserve Your Spot."

## Search

Every public page should have:
- A unique title
- A useful meta description
- A canonical URL using `https://ggpumpkins.com`
- Crawlable links in normal anchor tags
- Descriptive image alt text
- Consistent business email and phone

Sitewide search helpers:
- `robots.txt`
- `sitemap.xml`
- LocalBusiness structured data on the home page

Core search phrases to support naturally:
- fall porch styling Hickory NC
- pumpkin porch display Hickory NC
- luxury fall porch styling
- porch pumpkins near Hickory
- Gorgeous Gourds

## Pages

Home:
- First impression should show the highest-end package with tasteful add-ons.
- H1 is "Gorgeous Gourds."
- Clear subheading: luxury pumpkin porch styling, delivered, styled, removed.

Packages:
- Make pricing and package differences easy to scan.
- Use premium porch imagery behind the page title.

Gallery:
- Use realistic package examples and before/after imagery.
- Do not include internal image-direction notes on the customer-facing page.

FAQ:
- Answer booking, timing, service area, pumpkin lifespan, deposit, customization, and removal questions plainly.

Contact:
- Make text, email, booking timing, and deposit action easy to find.
