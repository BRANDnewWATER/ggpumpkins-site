Why Lincolnton Homeowners Choose Gorgeous Gourds

The historic core radiates out from Courthouse Square, with stately homes along West Main Street and through the South Aspen Street district — porches that deserve a display built to their proportions. We tailor the mix of pumpkin sizes, varieties, and colors to your porch's width, step count, and style, whether you're near the square or in one of Lincolnton's newer neighborhoods.

We source directly from local North Carolina farms — fresh pumpkins, not warehouse stock — so your display looks full and vibrant from installation day through Thanksgiving. After we finish, we send you a photo of the completed porch. You don't have to be home.
