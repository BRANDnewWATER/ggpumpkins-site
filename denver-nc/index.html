Why Denver Homeowners Choose Gorgeous Gourds

From the custom homes and tree-lined streets of Sailview to the resort-style community at Trilogy and the established neighborhoods off NC-16 like Westport, Denver porches tend to be generous — deep, wide, and made for a full, layered display. We tailor the mix of pumpkin sizes, varieties, and colors to your porch's proportions and your home's style, so the finished look belongs on a lake home, not a template.

We source directly from local North Carolina farms — fresh pumpkins, not warehouse stock — so your display looks full and vibrant from installation day through Thanksgiving. After we finish, we send you a photo of the completed porch. You don't have to be home.
