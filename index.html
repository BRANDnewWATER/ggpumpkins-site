Common Questions

Quick answers before you book

How long do the pumpkins last?

Pumpkins on a covered porch last 4–8 weeks, much longer than ones sitting in direct sun or landscaping. We source fresh from local farms right before your install date so they look great for the full season.

Do I need to be home for installation?

Nope. We work on your front porch while you're out. Just make sure we have access to the porch area. We'll send you a photo of the finished display once we're done.

What's the $100 deposit for?

The $100 deposit reserves your spot on our calendar and is applied toward your total balance. Remaining balance is due at installation. Deposits are refundable if you cancel 14+ days before your scheduled install.
